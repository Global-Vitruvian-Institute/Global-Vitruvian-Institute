# Reflection

Five minutes of writing about what you intend and why.

## What to do

Set a timer for five minutes and write without editing. Answer one question: what do you want from this week, and what would make that worth it? Plain sentences are enough. Stop when the timer stops.

## What to notice

Which intentions are yours and which arrived from someone else. Which ones you could act on this week.

## Prompt

If nothing else went well this week, what would still make it a good week?
